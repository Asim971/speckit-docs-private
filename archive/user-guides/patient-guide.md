# Patient Quick Start Guide

Welcome to JibonFlow! This guide will help you get started with Bangladesh's most comprehensive digital health platform. Access quality healthcare from anywhere with secure telemedicine consultations and medicine delivery.

## 🏥 What is JibonFlow?

JibonFlow connects patients with qualified healthcare providers through:
- **Secure Video Consultations** - HIPAA-compliant telemedicine optimized for 3G networks
- **Medicine Delivery** - Order medications directly from verified pharmacies
- **Health Records** - Digital medical records accessible anytime
- **Community Health Workers** - Local CHW support in rural areas

### Step 2: Create Your Account

2. **Tap "Sign Up"**

5. **Complete Your Profile**:

   - Add emergency contact

   - Policy number

   - Doctor gender preference

## 🎥 Booking Your First Consultation

### Finding a Doctor

1. **Open "Find Doctor"** tab
2. **Choose Specialty**:
   - General Practice (সাধারণ চিকিৎসা)
   - Cardiology (হৃদরোগ)
   - Pediatrics (শিশু চিকিৎসা)
   - Gynecology (স্ত্রীরোগ)
   - Dermatology (চর্মরোগ)
   - And 15+ more specialties

3. **Filter by**:
   - **Language**: Bengali, English, Hindi
   - **Experience**: Years of practice
   - **Rating**: Patient reviews (1-5 stars)
   - **Availability**: Today, Tomorrow, This Week
   - **Consultation Fee**: 200-2000 BDT

4. **View Doctor Profile**:
   - Education and qualifications
   - Hospital affiliations
   - Patient reviews
   - Available time slots

### Booking Appointment

1. **Select Doctor** and tap "Book Appointment"
2. **Choose Date & Time**:
   - Available slots shown in real-time
   - Morning: 9 AM - 12 PM
   - Afternoon: 2 PM - 6 PM
   - Evening: 7 PM - 10 PM

3. **Select Consultation Type**:
   - **Video Call** (recommended): Full video consultation
   - **Audio Call**: Voice-only consultation
   - **Chat Only**: Text-based consultation

4. **Add Consultation Notes**:
   - Main symptoms
   - Duration of symptoms
   - Previous treatments tried
   - Upload photos if relevant

5. **Choose Payment Method**:
   - **bKash**: Mobile payment
   - **Nagad**: Mobile payment
   - **Rocket**: Mobile payment
   - **Credit/Debit Card**: Visa, MasterCard
   - **Bank Transfer**: Online banking

6. **Confirm Booking**:
   - Review appointment details
   - Agree to terms and conditions
   - Tap "Confirm & Pay"

### Payment Confirmation

- **Instant confirmation** after successful payment
- **SMS notification** with appointment details
- **Email confirmation** with video call link
- **Calendar reminder** added automatically

## 📞 Joining Your Video Consultation

### 15 Minutes Before Appointment

1. **Check Your Connection**:
   - Ensure stable internet (3G or better)
   - Close other apps to save bandwidth
   - Charge your device to 50%+

2. **Prepare Your Environment**:
   - Find quiet, well-lit space
   - Clean camera lens
   - Test microphone and speaker
   - Have pen and paper ready

3. **Get Notification**:
   - Push notification 15 mins before
   - SMS reminder with join link
   - Tap "Join Consultation" when ready

### During the Consultation

#### Joining the Call

1. **Tap "Join Consultation"** from notification or app
2. **Allow Permissions**:
   - Camera access
   - Microphone access
   - Notification access

3. **Test Audio/Video**:
   - Check camera angle
   - Test microphone
   - Adjust volume

4. **Wait for Doctor**:
   - You'll see "Waiting for doctor..."
   - Doctor will join within 2-3 minutes
   - Stay connected and don't close app

#### During the Video Call

**Doctor's Video Screen**:
- Doctor appears in main video window
- Your video appears in small corner
- Tap to switch views

**Control Buttons**:
- 🎤 **Mute/Unmute**: Control your microphone
- 📹 **Video On/Off**: Turn camera on/off
- 💬 **Chat**: Send text messages during call
- 📱 **Screen Share**: Share photos or documents
- ❌ **End Call**: Leave consultation

**Features Available**:
- **Real-time Chat**: Type messages if audio unclear
- **Photo Sharing**: Share symptom photos, test results
- **Screen Sharing**: Show medicine bottles, documents
- **Call Recording**: Automatic for your records (with consent)

#### Best Practices During Call

✅ **Do**:
- Speak clearly in your preferred language
- Describe symptoms in detail
- Ask questions about treatment
- Take notes of doctor's advice
- Show relevant documents or photos

❌ **Don't**:
- Move around too much (keeps video stable)
- Have multiple people talking
- Use speakerphone in noisy area
- Forget to mention current medications

### After Consultation

1. **Receive Summary**:
   - Digital prescription (if prescribed)
   - Consultation notes
   - Doctor's recommendations
   - Follow-up instructions

2. **Rate Your Experience**:
   - Rate doctor (1-5 stars)
   - Leave review for other patients
   - Report any technical issues

3. **Access Records**:
   - All consultations saved in "My Records"
   - Download prescription PDF
   - Share with family members if needed

## 💊 Ordering Medicine

### From Prescription

1. **After Consultation**:
   - Prescription automatically saved
   - Tap "Order Medicine" button
   - All prescribed medicines pre-added to cart

2. **Choose Pharmacy**:
   - See nearby pharmacies on map
   - Compare prices and ratings
   - Check delivery time estimates
   - Select preferred pharmacy

3. **Review Order**:
   - Verify medicine names and dosages
   - Check generic alternatives (save money)
   - Add over-the-counter medicines if needed
   - Apply insurance/discount codes

4. **Delivery Options**:
   - **Home Delivery**: 2-4 hours in Dhaka, next day outside
   - **Pharmacy Pickup**: Ready in 30-60 minutes
   - **Express Delivery**: 1-2 hours (extra fee)

### Without Prescription (OTC)

1. **Browse Medicine Catalog**:
   - Search by name or category
   - Pain relievers, vitamins, first aid
   - Baby care, women's health
   - All medicines verified for authenticity

2. **Medicine Information**:
   - Generic and brand names
   - Uses and dosage instructions
   - Side effects and warnings
   - Price comparison across pharmacies

3. **QR Code Verification**:
   - Scan medicine QR code
   - Verify authenticity
   - Check expiry date
   - See manufacturing details

### Payment & Delivery

**Payment Methods**:
- Same as consultation payments
- **Cash on Delivery** available
- **Insurance Coverage** where applicable

**Delivery Tracking**:
- Real-time GPS tracking
- SMS updates on delivery status
- Estimated delivery time
- Direct contact with delivery person

**Quality Assurance**:
- All medicines verified authentic
- Cold chain maintained for vaccines
- Expiry date guaranteed 6+ months
- Return policy for damaged items
