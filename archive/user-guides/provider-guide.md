## 🎥 Conducting Video Consultations

### Pre-Consultation Preparation

#### 15 Minutes Before Consultation

**Technology Check**:
- Test camera and microphone
- Verify internet connection (minimum 2 Mbps)
- Close unnecessary applications
- Enable "Do Not Disturb" mode

**Environment Setup**:
- Professional background or virtual backdrop
- Adequate lighting (face clearly visible)
- Minimize background noise
- Have notepad and pen ready

**Patient File Review**:
- Read patient's medical history
- Review presenting complaints
- Check previous consultation notes
- Prepare relevant questions

#### Patient Preparation Verification

**Technology Readiness**:
- Patient has completed tech check
- Camera and microphone working
- Stable internet connection
- JibonFlow app updated

**Medical Preparation**:
- Symptoms documentation completed
- Current medications listed
- Previous reports uploaded
- Emergency contact information verified

### During the Consultation

#### Joining the Video Call

1. **Start Consultation**:
   - Click "Join Consultation" 2-3 minutes early
   - Test audio/video before patient joins
   - Review patient notes one final time

2. **Patient Connection**:
   - Greet patient professionally
   - Verify patient identity
   - Confirm consultation preferences (language, etc.)
   - Explain consultation process

3. **Technical Setup**:
   - Ensure both parties have clear audio/video
   - Test screen sharing functionality
   - Verify secure connection indicator
   - Enable recording if consented

#### Consultation Best Practices

**Professional Communication**:
- Use patient's preferred name and language
- Speak clearly and at appropriate pace
- Maintain eye contact with camera
- Use professional medical terminology with explanations

**Clinical Assessment**:
- **History Taking**:
  - Chief complaint and history of present illness
  - Past medical and surgical history
  - Current medications and allergies
  - Family and social history
  - Review of systems

- **Physical Examination** (Limited):
  - Visual inspection of accessible areas
  - Guide patient for self-examination
  - Use smartphone flashlight for better visibility
  - Request assistance from family member if needed

- **Diagnostic Assessment**:
  - Formulate differential diagnosis
  - Determine need for additional tests
  - Assess urgency and appropriate care level
  - Plan follow-up requirements

**Documentation During Call**:
- Use built-in consultation notes feature
- Record key findings and observations
- Note patient's understanding and compliance
- Document any teaching provided

#### Advanced Consultation Features

**Screen Sharing**:
- Share educational materials
- Review test results with patient
- Demonstrate exercises or procedures
- Show medication information

**Photo/Document Review**:
- Patient can share photos of symptoms
- Review lab reports and imaging
- Examine skin conditions or injuries
- Verify medication bottles

**Multi-Party Consultations**:
- Include family member or caregiver
- Interpreter services when needed
- Specialist referral consultations
- Care team coordination meetings

**Chat Integration**:
- Send text messages during call
- Share links to resources
- Provide written instructions
- Answer follow-up questions

### Post-Consultation Activities

#### Immediate Follow-up (Within 5 minutes)

**Consultation Summary**:
- Complete clinical documentation
- Record diagnosis and assessment
- Document treatment plan
- Note any referrals or follow-up needed

**Prescription Management**:
- Create digital prescriptions if needed
- Verify drug interactions and allergies
- Select appropriate pharmacies for delivery
- Set refill schedules and instructions

**Patient Communication**:
- Send consultation summary to patient
- Provide written care instructions
- Schedule follow-up appointments if needed
- Share relevant educational resources

#### Documentation Requirements

**SOAP Note Format**:
```
Subjective:
- Chief complaint
- History of present illness
- Review of systems
- Patient's perspective and concerns

Objective:
- Vital signs (if available)
- Physical examination findings
- Telemedicine-specific observations
- Patient appearance and behavior

Assessment:
- Primary diagnosis
- Differential diagnosis
- Risk assessment
- Prognosis and complications

Plan:
- Treatment recommendations
- Medications prescribed
- Follow-up instructions
- Patient education provided
- Referrals if needed
```

**Quality Assurance**:
- Complete consultation within scheduled time
- Address all patient concerns
- Ensure patient understanding
- Document patient satisfaction

## 🛠️ Technical Support & Troubleshooting

### Common Technical Issues

#### Video Consultation Problems

**Poor Video Quality**:
- Check internet bandwidth (minimum 2 Mbps)
- Close unnecessary applications
- Move closer to Wi-Fi router
- Switch to mobile data if Wi-Fi unstable

**Audio Issues**:
- Verify microphone permissions
- Check system audio settings
- Test with different browsers
- Use headset for better audio quality

**Connection Drops**:
- Stable internet connection required
- Avoid peak internet usage times
- Use ethernet connection when possible
- Keep backup mobile data ready
